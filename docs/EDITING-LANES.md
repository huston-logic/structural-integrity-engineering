# What you can change on your site

Your site is built from sections. You can change most of it yourself, by chatting with your AI. Some things need
Huston Logic. This page shows which is which.

Each item has one mark:

- **Works today**: you can ask for this now.
- **Coming**: planned, not available yet.
- **Huston Logic builds it**: this needs design, setup or a build by Huston Logic first.

You will be able to change your site by chatting with your AI app. Huston Logic has not turned this on for your site yet.
Once it is on, you sign in at `https://structural-integrity-engineering.com/owner`. That page shows the changes waiting
for your yes, your recent changes (with Withdraw or Undo), and how to connect your AI app.

## How a change works

1. Ask your AI app for the change in plain words.
2. Read the list it shows. It lists exactly what will change, with the old and new words.
3. Say yes. The change goes live in a few minutes (about 5, after the same checks as every other change). Until your
   AI app says it is live, it is not.
4. Ask "is it live yet?" to check.
5. Ask "undo that" to take a change back (a change still being checked is withdrawn instead). The undo shows a list
   too, and you say yes to it.
6. Anything outside these lanes is passed to Huston Logic.

## Lane 1: Do it yourself by chatting

Ask for any of these in plain words.

- **Change any words.** Headings, paragraphs, buttons, questions and answers. Works today.
- **Swap or add photos** where a section takes one. Works today. Upload the photos on your owner page (under Your
  uploads), then ask your AI app to use them. Give each photo a short description of what it shows. The description is
  for people who cannot see the photo.
- **Offer a PDF to download**, such as a price sheet. Works today. Upload the PDF on your owner page, then ask your AI
  app for a button or a link to it.
- **Add, remove or reorder sections** on a page, using the sections listed below. Works today.
- **Add a new page** made from those sections. Works today.
- **Archive a page.** An archived page leaves the site (Huston Logic takes it out of the menu). Nothing is deleted, and you can ask for the page
  back at any time. Works today.
- **Change prices, contact details and your privacy text** written in your pages. These are your business facts.
  Huston Logic does not review them before they go live. Works today. (The email address and booking link the whole
  site uses, in the header, footer and contact form, are site settings: see Lane 3.)
- **Add a YouTube or Vimeo video**, or a grid of videos. Works today. Upload the video to YouTube or Vimeo first, not to
  your site: uploads refuse video files.
- **Write blog posts.** Ask for a post in plain words, in your voice. A new post is saved as a draft. Ask to publish it,
  archive it or restore it. A post has its own page, is listed on your blog and is in your feed. Works today.
- **Show your latest posts** on any page, or list every post on your blog page. Works today.
- **Add videos** to your videos, from YouTube or Vimeo, and show them in a list. Works today.
- **Add podcast episodes** from Spotify or Apple Podcasts, and show them in a list. Works today.
- **Add testimonials.** You give the quote and the name of the person who said it. The AI app never writes a quote or
  a name you did not give it. Works today.
- **Add team members.** A name, a role, an optional bio and an optional photo. Works today.
- **Change your business details**: phone number, email address, address, opening hours and social links. They show in
  the header and footer and on any page with a business details section. Works today.
- **Put up a site notice**: one short line at the top of every page, with an optional link and an optional last day.
  Take it down whenever you like. Works today.
- **New forms.** Ask for a form, such as a quote request or a sign-up, with the fields you need, and place it on a page. Works today.
- **File-download forms.** A visitor leaves their email address to get a PDF you uploaded. Works today.

### The check on every change

Every change is checked before it goes live. If something is wrong, the check says what to fix, in plain words. For
example:

- A link points to a page that does not exist.
- A section has more items than it can hold.
- A video link is not from YouTube or Vimeo.
- A photo has no description, or its description is only a file name.
- A photo still holds hidden camera or location data.
- A PDF holds a script or an attached file.

### Your sections

Each section below is one block you can place on a page. The list says what each one is for.

<!-- blocks:start -->
- **Hero**: The top of a page: a small line above, the page headline, a short introduction and, if wanted, one button.
- **Highlights**: A heading and a set of short ideas, each with a title and a few lines of text, and optionally a photo above each. Good for services, reasons or features. (You can choose a look: **two-column**, the ideas sit in two columns, one column on phones, with soft dividers; **three-column**, the ideas sit in three columns on wide screens, with three or more ideas, two on tablets, one on phones.)
- **Text section**: A heading with a few paragraphs under it. Good for a story, a background or a longer explanation.
- **Questions and answers**: A heading and a list of questions that open to show their answers.
- **Closing call to action**: The last block on a page: a heading, a line of text and one button that says what to do next.
- **Contact form**: A booking column next to the site's contact form. The form itself is wired to the site's own settings.
- **Form**: One of the site's forms (a quote request, a download sign-up) in a panel, with an optional heading and a sentence above or beside it. The form's fields, button and thank-you message are in its own form file. (You can choose a look: **stacked**, the heading and intro above the form panel, in one centred column; **beside**, the heading and intro on the left, the form panel on the right, stacked on phones.)
- **Numbered steps**: A heading and a short numbered list that says what happens, step by step. It can end with an email line.
- **Policy sections**: A run of titled sections for a policy or terms page, with an optional line at the end such as a last-updated date.
- **Video**: One video from YouTube or Vimeo, with a title and a short caption. It plays on the page when a visitor presses play. (You can choose a look: **wide**, as wide as the page column; **inset**, narrower and centered, the width of a reading column.)
- **Video grid**: Two to six videos from YouTube or Vimeo side by side, each with a title and a short caption. (You can choose a look: **two-column**, the videos sit in two columns, one column on phones; **three-column**, the videos sit in three columns on wide screens, with three or more videos, two on tablets, one on phones.)
- **Photo with text**: One photo beside a heading, a few paragraphs and an optional button. Good for an about section, a place or a service. (You can choose a look: **photo-start**, the photo sits on the left on wide screens, the text on the right. On phones the photo is above the text; **photo-end**, the photo sits on the right on wide screens, the text on the left. On phones the photo is above the text.)
- **Photo gallery**: Two to twelve photos in a grid, each with an optional caption. The photos do not open larger. (You can choose a look: **three-column**, the photos sit three across on wide screens and two across on tablets and phones; **two-column**, the photos sit two across on wide screens and tablets, and one across on phones.)
- **Latest posts**: The newest blog posts, with a heading and an optional link to all of them. It updates by itself when a post is added, and is left out of the page while there is no post. (You can choose a look: **cards**, each post is a card with its photo on top, a tinted panel with the date when it has no photo, then the date, title and summary; **list**, each post is a row with its date, title and summary, divided by soft lines.)
- **Post index**: The list of every blog post, newest first, ten to a page, with links to newer and older pages. It belongs on the blog page only, once. (You can choose a look: **list**, each post is a row with its date, title and summary, divided by soft lines; **cards**, the posts are cards in a grid, each with its photo on top, a tinted panel with the date when it has no photo.)
- **Business details**: A heading and the business's address, phone, email and opening hours, read from the business details settings (business.json), so they are written once and stay the same everywhere. Parts that are empty are left out. (You can choose a look: **columns**, address, contact and hours side by side on wide screens, stacked on phones; **compact**, address, contact and hours in one narrow column.)
- **Video list**: The newest videos from your Videos collection, side by side, each with its title and caption. Add videos in the collection; this block shows them. (You can choose a look: **two-column**, the videos sit in two columns, one column on phones; **three-column**, the videos sit in three columns on wide screens, with three or more videos, two on tablets, one on phones.)
- **Podcast episodes**: The newest episodes from your Podcast episodes collection, each with its date and summary. Add episodes in the collection; this block shows them. (You can choose a look: **list**, one row per episode with its player beside the text on wide screens, below it on phones; **compact**, one row per episode with its date and a link that opens the episode on Spotify or Apple Podcasts. No player.)
- **Testimonials**: Quotes from named clients, taken from your Testimonials collection in the order you set there. Every quote shows who said it. (You can choose a look: **grid**, the quotes sit in cards, two across on tablets and three across on wide screens, one column on phones; **feature**, the first quote is large, in the accent serif. Any others sit in a row beneath it.)
- **Team**: The people from your Team collection, in the order you set there, with a photo, name, role and short bio. (You can choose a look: **grid**, a card per person with a tall photo, a tinted panel with their initials when there is no photo, name, role and bio; **list**, names and roles in two columns, no photos.)
- **Home hero**: The home page's first screen: a centered headline, a line under it and a button, in the sky of a dusk photo with an amber line drawing on the house.
- **Photo hero**: A page top with the headline and a line on the left and a photo on the right that carries the page's amber line drawing.
- **Service page hero**: The top of a service page: the headline and a line, the overview paragraph, and a hero photo that carries the page's amber line drawing.
- **Page hero**: A plain page top on dusk: the headline and a line, with an amber line drawing beside them if the page has one.
- **Contact page**: The whole contact page: the headline and a line with the direct email and phone, and the six-field form beside them. The form is wired to the site's own settings.
- **Design conditions**: A deep band with a heading and a line, then up to six short items in columns, each under a rule with its own amber glyph.
- **Photo cards**: A heading with a line beside it, then up to six cards: a tall photo, a title that links to a page and a short text.
- **Service choices**: A deep band with a heading and up to six linked cards, each a photo, a title and a short text.
- **Figures and reasons**: One deep band: big amber figures that count up as they scroll into view, then a heading and a grid of short reasons.
- **Heading and dot list**: A heading in a narrow left column with a list of short points beside it, each with an amber dot. (You can choose a look: **dusk**, on the page's dusk color; **deep**, on the deeper band color.)
- **Heading and paragraph**: A heading in a narrow left column with one paragraph beside it, on the deeper band color.
- **Numbered steps**: A heading and a numbered list of steps in two columns, then a second heading with a paragraph under a divider.
- **Questions beside a heading**: A heading in a narrow left column with questions that open to show their answers beside it.
- **What we do**: A deep band with a heading and a ruled list of the work, each with an optional line of detail, and one or two photos beside it.
- **How we work, short**: A heading that links to the full steps, with the first step written out beside it.
- **Statement**: One large paragraph on a deep band: a single statement of what the practice is.
- **Profile**: A portrait photo beside a heading and a paragraph about a person.
- **Standard and mission**: One deep band: a heading over a wide photo with a line of licensing under it, then a divider and the mission.
- **Policy sections**: Plain headed sections of text in a narrow reading column, then a closing line such as the date the policy was last updated.
- **Contact band**: The band that ends a page: a centered headline, a line, the Request a quote button, then the phone number and email from the site settings.
